<h2>Where CiteSite stands apart</h2>
<h3>Built for the citation era, not the keyword era</h3><p>Every tool in this comparison was originally built to improve rankings in Google Search. CiteSite's audit is designed around a different question: does your page give AI systems enough structured, authoritative signal to cite you in an answer? That difference runs through every dimension of the score.</p>
<h3>Schema Forge — the only built-in markup generator</h3><p>None of the tools above generate structured data for you. CiteSite includes <a href="https://citesite.net/schema-forge">Schema Forge</a>, a free AI-powered generator that reads any URL and returns a copy-paste-ready <code>&lt;script type="application/ld+json"&gt;</code> block — auto-detecting Article, FAQ, HowTo, Product, and Event schemas. Available free to newsletter subscribers.</p>
<h3>A human reads every paid report</h3><p>Every full audit is reviewed by an analyst before delivery. You get findings you can act on, not a raw data dump that requires its own interpretation.</p>
<h3>One price, one report</h3><p>There is no subscription. Run an audit when you need one — per site, per sprint, per client. At CHF 49.99 per report, the full cost is recovered in less than half a day of what the cheapest competitor charges per month.</p>
<h2>Bottom line</h2><p>If your priority is traditional SEO — backlink monitoring, keyword rank tracking at scale, or site-wide technical crawling — Semrush and Ahrefs remain capable tools for that job. If you want to understand and improve how AI search engines read your content, CiteSite is the only tool in this list that was built for that purpose.</p>
